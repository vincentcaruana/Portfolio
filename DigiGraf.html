Transcendence

Motion Design, Sound Design, Installation, Touchdesigner, Adobe Premiere Pro, Ableton

This piece was created for Digital Graffiti, Alys Beach’s projection mapping festival. I aimed to create a captivating, ethereal experience that encouraged viewers to slow down, be present, and take in the moment.

Each visual sequence was created in TouchDesigner and then compiled into one long video in Adobe Premiere Pro, while the music was composed in Ableton. This project required a lot of expirimentation to get a satisfying result, but I am very happy with how it turned out!
